Spec 046 · Round 4 · Vibe frames

A day in Worktown

Six frames about how the world feels, not how one model looks: the robots that keep the town tidy, the machines you buy when you grow, what guests see from their table, a rush with the whole crew, a cook-off at night, and the town around it all.

These are concept frames: quick scenes from the lab's kit with ink outlines, so nobody mistakes them for final art. Click a frame to see it large.

Morning · before opening

The town wakes up

Little sweeper robots clean the pavement, a runner robot washes a shop window, hens wander across, a neighbour walks the dog. The cooks stand in their doors.

  • Animals are neighbours here, not food (D27).
  • Robots do the chores nobody wants; people do the cooking.
  • A calm start before the rush.
Service · the guest's seat

What your guests see

From a seat: your plate in front, booths and round tables full, a robot runner bringing the next order, and behind the pass your friends cooking under the menu board.

  • Sit-down tables are a stage 3 service.
  • The room is where status shows: it gets nicer as you climb.
Service · the rush

Everyone in the kitchen

Two stove lines, a prep island, the pass with plates going out, tickets everywhere, steam, mess building up. Six friends, each finding their spot.

  • Roles are free: the work makes them natural.
  • The mess is the cleaning gameplay from round 3.
Stage 4 · automation

The machines you earn

A wall of little windows with a dish in each, a robot arm stirring the pot, a conveyor feeding the wall. The crew moves from doing everything to running the place.

  • An idea from the retro future: the automat restaurant.
  • Machines are bought with money you earned, never sold for real money.
Stage 5 · events

The cook-off

Night, string lights, a crowd. Your stall against the Golden Spoon, a spotless rival chain with matching hats. A split-flap scoreboard: 87 to 91. Three judges, score cards up.

  • Big, planned jobs with big payouts: the heist feeling.
  • Rivals have names, faces and a look of their own.
The world

Where food comes from

A glass garden dome where the town grows its food, a street of small restaurants with flats and roof gardens above, a monorail gliding past, searchlights at dusk.

  • The dome is the vegan town's answer to the farms of the dark past.
  • Your restaurant is one of many lights on this street.

The climb

  1. Food cornerone dish, cash only
  2. First restaurantrent, menu, card
  3. Your mixtables, delivery, buffet
  4. Automationstaff and machines
  5. Beyond the kitchencook-offs, events, fame

The two strips are the same loop at two speeds: every day earns money and reputation, and that money buys the next step of the climb. The town around you (robots, neighbours, the dome, rivals) stays the same calm, friendly place, so growth is the change you see.
