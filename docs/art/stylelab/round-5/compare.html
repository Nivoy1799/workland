Worktown 2222

The same friendly retro-future, two hundred years on: garden towers with ribbon windows and terraces, sky bridges, hover cars in lanes, delivery drones with parcels, an airship over the fair. Our little restaurant street stays at the foot of it all.

Your street · sunset

Small shops, big city

Casa Nopal, Tomorrow Kitchen, Yuzu Noodles and the flower shop at street level; behind them a garden courtyard, then the towers: ribbon windows, ribbed Art Deco shafts, terrace trees, glass garden domes on top.

  • The monorail glides past at roof height.
  • Hover cars fly in lanes, drones carry parcels, the fair airship hangs in the haze.
Your street · night

The city lights up

Window bands glow, shop signs turn neon, hover cars draw short light trails, searchlights sweep the clouds. The red night of the 1930s painting, in our town.

Morning · car-free

The garden promenade

Cars fly, so the ground belongs to people and plants: raised beds with trees and flowers, a fountain, café tables in front of the shops, a hen wandering, a sweeper robot, drones bringing breakfast.

  • Outdoor tables could be one of your services.
  • The promenade is where guests choose between you and your rivals.
Stage 3 · delivery

Delivery from the roof

Your own drone pad marked TK, parcels with order numbers, a drone landing for the next one. Herb beds and a little greenhouse on the same roof: the kitchen grows its own garnish.

  • Drones are the 2222 version of the delivery service.
  • The roof garden could feed the ingredient lab in stage 5.
The world

A garden with towers in it

The old town street and the garden dome in a park, ringed by towers, a monorail loop and flying lanes. This is the weakest frame of the round: from up here the kit looks like a model railway, so it shows the layout more than the mood.

How 2222 fits the game. The future is the backdrop; the restaurant stays human-sized. Towers, drones and hover cars make the world feel big and alive, and you still start with a cart on the pavement. Climbing means your place gets a drone pad, a roof garden, a van in the flying lane, and maybe one day the sky-deck on top of a tower.
