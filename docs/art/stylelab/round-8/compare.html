Haven Nine

The town now has hard borders and a reason for them. One high glass Dome covers the whole town, a black Storm Ring stands on every horizon, and each edge of the map is the same wall told a different way. The town also gets its shaded side: the Low Ring.

A lifeboat that worked too well: the walls that keep everyone safe also keep everyone in.

The Dome

Higher than every tower

From outside

The Dome is a bubble, not a lid: steep at the foot, round at the top, its crown 250 m up and leaning a little west. Main ribs run from the crown to the ground and branch like a leaf's veins. Outside it there is only bare land and dark water, and on the horizon the black Storm Ring. Inside: everything you know from round 6.

From below, at night

The look you liked from below, at night. The ribs are lit, so the whole sky becomes a lamp shade over the town. This is also where night play happens: deliveries, the night market, bars.

The borders

Where the world ends

Hard stops, each with a reason. The invisible wall and the server's safe-spot check stay behind them as the last resort.

West: the Wreck

The wall was built straight through the hull and sealed with a riveted collar. The tail and the market are inside; the nose lies outside in the storm. You can walk the ship as far as the welded bulkhead at the wall.

East: the lake

The wall runs down into the water. Water gate 3 opens for the cleaning craft only. Inside the lake is calm and light; outside it is choppy and grey under the storm.

North: the tunnel

Once a season the sealed cargo train from Haven Eight comes through. Goods only: the board says "Transit closed. Next service: Autumn". Later this door could open for travel between Havens.

The Low Ring

Everything, but not a reason

The ground floors of the towers and the land at the Dome's feet lie in shade all day. Basics are free here too. What is missing is purpose.

Broken robotsNo spare parts come in, so the 2130s robots limp, loop old ads and end on the scrap pile. That is the mechanic's work later.
Full binsBroken collectors mean overflowing bins: a smell, fewer customers. Emptying them by hand is an easy first job.
Bored peopleBetting on drone races, credit slots, dream booths. Shown in the world and its stories, never a loop you grind. All made up.

Your kitchen is one of the few warm places down here. Hot soup, made by hand draws a queue in a town where printed food is free.

The story, draft 2

What changed in history

In docs/town-story.md. The past is the game's own named era, never real-world events.

  1. until about 2090
    The Smoke Years. The old world burned too much and the weather broke.the museum, dry jokes
  2. 2090s – 2110s
    The Weather Engines. Huge machines calm the sky. They half work: calm valleys, but the storms they push away pile up into the Storm Ring. The fix became a cage.the black wall on every horizon, the old engine mast on the ridge
  3. 2110s
    The Charter and the twelve Havens. Seed libraries, food printers, robots, the animals from the last farms. "No one in a Haven will ever go without."the Charter in the Town Hall, the poster in the Low Ring
  4. 14 Sep 2122
    Ark Flight 9. The Albatross comes through a gap in the storm that closes early and lands hard at the valley's edge. Everyone lives. Captain Ilse Marrow: "Landed. Sort of." Flights 6 and 11 never arrive anywhere.the Wreck, the memorial
  5. the first harvest
    Rosa's stew. The ship's cook, Rosa Vell, makes the first supper. The animals walk down the ramp and stay.the first recipe you learn, the Wildbelt
  6. 2120s – 2161
    The Dome. Built in stages, closed for good after the Hail Summer, straight through the Wreck."the Sail" up top, "the Lid" down below
  7. 2161 – today
    The Long Calm. The promise holds, but nothing comes in. Robots break, everything is reused, work is optional, real cooking matters.the whole town
  8. today
    An ordinary year. One sealed cargo train a season from Haven Eight. Rumours that the storm is thinning.the train hall, NPC talk

Inside the glass

Weather, night and compost

Planned weather

  • The Keepers hold a daily Rain Hour to water the town.
  • Roof panels open on fine days, so the seasons still come in.
  • Storms, hail and lightning hit the glass from outside.
  • Footfall follows the schedule: open-panel days fill outdoor tables, the Rain Hour empties them.
  • Night is never skipped (proposed): about 10 of the 40 minutes, played like the day.

Compost loop (proposed)

  1. Cooking makes scraps; the sim computes how much per recipe. They go in the back-door bin.
  2. Robots empty the bins on a route. Broken ones in the Low Ring leave bins full.
  3. Players can empty bins by hand for a little pay per kilo; in autumn, sweep leaves off the Dome.
  4. The Compost Works makes soil over a few days. Plenty of soil makes greenhouse food up to about 10% cheaper.
