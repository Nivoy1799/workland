The whole town, and where it ends

A town in a shallow bowl that opens east onto a lake. It climbs 45 m from the water to Greenhouse Hill, and every daily job is within a short walk. At each edge the world ends at something you can see and explain: a lake, a sanctuary, a wreck, a hill. Weather and daylight change both the look and the business.

Where the world ends

Four natural edges, never a bare wall

Each edge has a reason in the story and a soft way to turn you back. As a last resort there is an invisible collider 15 m behind each edge, and the host moves anyone who slips through to the nearest safe spot.

West · the Wreck

Your crashed plane, as an airship

The Albatross, a 2100s cargo airship, came down in a storm a hundred years ago and still lies across the valley mouth. Moss and trees grow on it, and a night market fills its belly. Behind it the canyon walls and a landslide close the valley. Next to it, the old highway ends in mid-air, a leftover from when cars drove.

  • A stage 5 event location, not just a barrier.
South · the Wildbelt

Animals only beyond this point

The old pasture land, given back to nature. Cows, pigs, deer and hens live there free, and the old barn is their shelter. A flowering hedge and a closed gate end the town. A ranger robot waves, and a lookout lets you watch.

  • It shows the peaceful present of D27, never the past.
East · the lake, in fog

Too far out, friend

The water fades into haze, the ferry only takes deliveries, and buoys mark the swim zone. Beyond them a red lifeguard drone tows you back. In this frame a food cart on the pier (pitch C5) serves its first guests of a foggy morning.

North · over the hill (no picture yet)

Farm terraces and the glass dome. Above them a fence ("Robots at work") and the steep ridge, the big city's skyline peeking over it. The monorail disappears into a tunnel whose gate opens for trains only. Up in the sky, players never fly: stage 5 cars stay on their lanes.

Weather and light

Weather that changes the business

The town's Weather Office schedules rain at night for the gardens and posts tomorrow's forecast, so you can plan stock. Now and then wild weather comes over the Wildbelt hills. Weather is fixed by the save's seed and the day: the host decides it, and clients draw it.

Storm · Main Street

Lightning hits the rods, never people

Heavy rain, glossy streets and puddles that mirror the neon. People carry umbrellas, and the drones are grounded, so a courier robot carries the parcel on foot. Lightning strikes the rod on a tower.

  • Fewer walk-up guests, more delivery orders, outdoor tables closed.
  • Rain washes the street, but guests carry mud inside: more cleaning.

Day and night. The host runs one town clock, and it runs only while the game runs. The proposal is 40 real minutes per day (24 day, 3 dusk, 10 night, 3 dawn). At dusk the street lamps come on first, then the shop signs and windows in a wave. Night never goes pitch dark, so older eyes can still read the street. Comfort settings: lightning flash from full to off, rain loudness, less camera rain.
