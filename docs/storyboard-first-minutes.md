# Storyboard: the first 3 minutes (DRAFT, 2026-11-10)

Status: draft, not decided. Written so it can also be used as picture prompts. Each shot lists what is visible, the
mood, and the message (from docs/setting.md) it carries. No dialogue; only signs, sounds and short exclamations.

## Shot 1: The street, morning (0:00–0:15)
A wide shot of a retro-future street in 2222:
- pastel buildings with rounded corners, chrome trim, big neon signs;
- a flying car with tailfins glides past high up, and a little delivery capsule shoots through a transparent pneumatic
  tube along the houses;
- a friendly dome-headed robot sweeps the pavement, and another waters a flower box.
- Warm morning light; low-poly, flat-shaded.

*Message 1: robots could do everything.*

## Shot 2: The food printer (0:15–0:30)
- At the street corner stands a grey, boxy **food printer** with a small screen saying "FREE".
- An NPC presses a button; with a "plop" a beige bar comes out. They look at it, shrug and eat it without joy.
- A second NPC walks past it, sniffing the air and looking somewhere else.

*Message 2 begins: free, but bland.*

## Shot 3: Your food corner (0:30–1:00)
- Across the street: an **old, slightly dusty food cart** with a hand-painted sign ("Joe's Corner"), a small griddle,
  copper pipes, a steaming pot.
- A clunky robot helper stands next to it and holds out a knife to you; you take it.
- Ingredient crate: "hand-grown by Mira, field 7".
- The player's first view (first person): the cart, the knife, one potato.

*Message 2: you could let the robot do it, but you want to do it yourself.*

## Shot 4: The first guest (1:00–1:45)
- The NPC from shot 2 comes over and smells your food. Their speech bubble: "Real food?"
- You cook the one dish. The guest takes a **retro coin purse** out of their pocket; coins clink on the counter, and
  the cash box goes "ka-ching". The tip jar gets its first coin.

*Message 4: cash is a ritual. Message 3: credits come from joy given.*

## Shot 5: The queue and the board (1:45–2:30)
- Two more guests line up; one films the food. The food printer across the street stands alone.
- Down the street, a **rankings board** (chrome, flip letters): "Top hobby kitchens this month: 1. Robo-Diner Deluxe ...
  27. Joe's Corner". Your name appears at the bottom.
- A rival team's stall further down has fans with pennants.

*Message 3: credits and rankings are a game people love.*

## Shot 6: The museum glimpse (2:30–3:00)
- Behind the board: a small museum with columns and a sign "Museum of the Old Era". Families walk in. Next to it a
  statue of people and animals together.
- No text beyond the sign. Back to your cart: the next guest is waiting.

*The world's history is there, but quiet (D27, D28).*

## What the player should be able to say after 3 minutes
"Robots do everything, but people love real hand-made food. I run a little food corner; guests pay in old-style
coins; there is a ranking to climb."
