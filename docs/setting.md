# Worktown setting (DRAFT, 2026-11-10, not decided)

Status: draft for the human to think about. Not binding. When decided it gets its own D number, and the palette (D32) and spec 040
follow it.

## The world in one sentence
**The year 2222, as people in 1950–80 imagined it: chrome, pastel and neon, robots everywhere, and still the best thing
in town is food made by human hands.**

## The four messages (shown, never explained)
1. **Robots could do everything.**
2. **People work out of passion; hand-made is precious.**
3. **Basics are free; credits are a game for the passionate.** Money option C: housing, energy and printed food cost
   nothing. Credits measure the joy you give and pay for your dreams (your own place, real ingredients, cars, events).
4. **Cash is a loved retro ritual.** Guests pay in coins and notes because that is part of a real diner visit.

Real, hand-grown ingredients are rare and cost credits; printed food is free but bland. That is why stock, prices and
suppliers exist.

## Look
- **Base: retro-futurism (atompunk, "Googie"):**
  - rounded shapes, boomerang and starburst motifs, tailfins;
  - chrome trim, terrazzo floors;
  - pastels (mint, salmon pink, cream, sky blue) with neon accents (cherry red, electric teal);
  - big sign typography, flying cars and delivery capsules, pneumatic tubes.
- **Seasoning: a little steampunk in the kitchen.** The old, hand-built machines (the spit, a coffee machine, the
  first robots) have brass and copper, valves and gauges, and visible steam. The kitchen feels crafted and warm; the
  street feels clean and futuristic.
- **Low-poly, flat-shaded** (D12, art-direction.md), the same rules for every model (spec 040 Part C).
- **Palette update** (D32): add mint, salmon, sky, chrome, neon cherry, neon teal and copper. Keep cream, wood and
  charcoal for warmth.

## Robots
- Clunky, friendly, 1950s style: dome heads, antennas, single wheel or tripod legs, tube arms, a little steam.
- They do the boring work perfectly: sweeping, carrying, washing up.
- In the game they are the stage 4 "automation" (D34): you buy robot helpers. The hand-made touch (the last fold of a
  wrap, the shave of the spit) stays human; robots never reach the top quality.

## People and humour
- People are relaxed, curious and proud of their hobbies. Rival teams have fan clubs and banners.
- Humour is light and dry: robots that try too hard, grey printer bars nobody wants, retro-mania (coin collectors,
  people who "work" for fun).
- The dark past (D27, D28) appears only as history: a museum, a statue, a plaque, a few NPC memories. Never preachy.

## Words
- **Use:** credits, hand-made, real food, regulars, the corner, fans, the printer (food printer), robots or bots.
- **Avoid:** salary, poverty, survival, wage slave; any real-world brand; real-world atrocities (D28).
