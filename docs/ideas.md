# Ideas (human wishes for later)

Not specs and not decisions. Each idea becomes a spec only when the human picks it. Newest first.

## Own a restaurant and choose how it serves (human, 2026-11-07)
- Buy or rent a restaurant in the town.
- Decide on a management level what kind of service it offers, also mixed:
  - fast food: order and pay at the counter, take the food away or eat in;
  - sit-down: guests sit at tables, a waiter takes the order at the table and brings the food;
  - delivery: orders come in and are delivered into the town;
  - buffet: the kitchen fills the buffet, customers pick what they want;
  - mixes of these (for example counter plus delivery).
- **Delivery becomes its own work simulation** later (a profession of its own: driving or riding
  through the town, picking up and dropping off orders), fitting the circular economy idea in
  AGENTS.md (one profession feeds another).
- Builds on: spec 022 (customer orders, shifts), spec 014 (customers), the economy specs 008/009.
  Likely needs: ownership and money in the backend (Postgres, Phase 4), restaurant settings that the
  server enforces, more NPC behaviour (tables, waiting, buffet picking).
