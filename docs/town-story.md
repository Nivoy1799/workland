# The town's story (DRAFT, 2026-11-11, not decided)

Status: a draft for the human, from spec 051's answer to question 7 ("we need some identity -> backstory"). Not
binding. It follows D27 (native vegan, people and animals live together) and D28: the past only as the game's
own history, a named era, no real-world events. The setting draft (`docs/setting.md`) sets the look: the year
2222 as 1950–80 imagined it.

Names in this file (the town, the ship, the people) are proposals. The three name ideas for the town are at the end.

## In one paragraph
A hundred years ago an old airship full of rescued animals and seeds came down in a storm in a quiet valley by a
lake. Nobody was hurt. The animals walked down the cargo ramp onto the meadow and stayed. The crew planted the seeds
on the slope, and the ship's cook made the first supper from the first harvest. They never left, and the town that
grew there is still proud of one thing above all: food made by hand. **The game starts in its hundredth year.**

## What the story gives the game
- **An identity in one line:** *a kitchen town that began with a crash, a cow and a stew.* Warm, a bit funny,
  never preachy.
- **Every landmark tells part of it:**
  - the Wreck is the landing;
  - the Wildbelt is the animals;
  - Greenhouse Hill is the seeds;
  - the Canopy is the ship's skin, grown over the town;
  - the museum keeps the captain's log and Rosa's stove.
- **The player's own start echoes it.** You arrive by monorail with one dish and a cart, as Rosa started with one
  stove. The first dishes in the game (potato hash, tomato stew) are a nod to the first supper.
- **The year has a goal.** Landing Day comes every autumn. The hundredth one is the big stage 5 event: the
  Centennial Supper at the Arena, where you cook against the best NPC teams. Every later year has its own Landing
  Day.
- **NPC lines, dry humour:**
  - "Grandma says the cows chose the town, not us."
  - "Landed. Sort of. Best thing ever written in a logbook."
  - "Don't tell the hens the market used to be their stable."
  - Robots: "I have computed Rosa's stew 4,000 times. It is never as good. I do not understand."
- **Seasons have a reason.** The Canopy came from a hail summer, and the leaves go back into the soil because the
  first settlers had only the seeds they carried. Nothing is thrown away: that is the circular economy, told as
  the town's habit.

## If another Wreck is picked (spec 051, question 10)
- **B, the Pelican freight plane:** the story stays the same, with Ark Flight 9 as a freight plane. A lucky belly
  landing on the meadow; the cargo door is the ramp.
- **C, the Stella starliner:** the Ark Flights become flights home from the Moon farms, carrying seeds and
  the animals born there. This is the most science fiction of the three, and the furthest from the cosy tone.

## Names (spec 051, question 11)
1. **Seedfall** (proposed): the seeds that fell from the sky. Short, easy to say in many languages, and it hints at
   autumn and growing.
2. **Albatross Landing**, "the Landing" for short: names the ship. Warm, but long.
3. **Kettlebay**: the town sits in a bowl (a kettle) by a bay, and it is a cooking town. Playful, but it says less
   about the story.

The game's own title stays open (O1). The town name can be the same as the game title or different.
