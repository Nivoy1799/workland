# The story of Haven Nine (DRAFT 3, 2026-11-11, for approval)

Status: draft 3, written for the v1 plan (`docs/town-plan.md`). It follows the human's answers in spec 051: the town
is **Haven Nine**, the Wreck is the airship **Albatross**, a high Dome covers the whole town, the town has **hard
borders** with a reason, the game starts in an ordinary year, and (new in draft 3) **the utopia has declined and the
players bring it back**. It follows D27 (native vegan) and D28 (the past only as the game's own named era).

Names of people are placeholders until the human decides (open question 1).

## The human's framing (2026-11-11, binding with D41 and D42)
- **Climate change** killed the Earth outside. People built the Domes for safety, and robots and automation made life
  inside a utopia, while the world outside died.
- **Everyone is vegan out of need**, not ethics: there was no other option left (D42).
- **The collapse:** it all broke apart. Nobody knew how to repair things any more, drugs and addiction came, and
  society broke down. That is the starting point of the game.
- **Start small:** Earth is like an unknown planet; you recultivate it from one small town.
- **Later:** you can explore the outside world, but only with oxygen and protection (D45).

The timeline below still needs to be aligned with these points (for example the Smoke Years as the climate collapse);
the next story pass does that.

## The idea in one line
**Haven Nine was a perfect shelter where nobody had to work. Fifty years on, nobody remembers how to keep it running.
You start with a cart and a crate of poor potatoes, and with every good meal the town comes back to life.**

## Timeline

| When | What happened | Where you see it today |
|---|---|---|
| **The Smoke Years** (until about 2090) | The named past era. The old world burned too much and the weather broke. | the museum, dry jokes |
| **The Weather Engines** (2090s–2110s) | Huge machines calm the sky. They half work: they make **calm valleys**, but the storms they push away pile up around them into the **Storm Ring**, a wall of storm that never stops. | the black cloud wall on every horizon |
| **The Charter and the Havens** (2110s) | Twelve calm valleys become **Havens**: seed libraries, food printers, robots, and the animals rescued from the last farms. The Charter promises: *"No one in a Haven will ever go without."* | the Charter in the Town Hall, the old posters |
| **14 September 2122** | **Ark Flight 9**, the airship **Albatross**, comes through a gap in the storm that closes early and lands hard at the valley's edge. Everyone lives. The animals walk down the ramp and stay. The ship's seed library becomes the town's first fields. | the Wreck, the Wildbelt |
| 2120s–2161 | The town builds **the Dome** and closes it for good after the Hail Summer of 2161. The wall runs straight through the Albatross. | the Dome; the hull sealed in the wall |
| **The Golden Calm** (2161 to about 2175) | The utopia works. Robots do every job, the printers make good food, nobody has to work. People paint, play, travel inside the Dome. | the faded murals, the grand empty restaurants, old people's stories |
| **The Long Fade** (about 2175 to today) | The last engineers retire and nobody learned their trade. Robots break one by one and stay broken. The printers' food turns grey and bland. The same seeds are replanted for fifty years and the crops get worse. With nothing to do and nothing good to eat, many people drift: betting halls, dream booths, long afternoons. The Charter still holds on paper: nobody goes hungry, but the paste is grey. | the whole town at stage 1: dead signs, slumped robots, the Low Ring |
| **Today** (an ordinary year, around 2222) | A few people decide to cook again, by hand, from what still grows. That is the players. | your cart |

## How the game tells it: the revival
The town you start in is tired and grey. As your restaurant climbs (game-design v1, D34), the town around you
changes for the better. The detailed rules are in `docs/town-plan.md`; in story terms:

| Climb stage | What the town looks like |
|---|---|
| 1 Food corner | grey: dead signs, broken robots, printer paste, poor potatoes; your cart is the one warm light |
| 2 First restaurant | first repairs: a lamp on again, a queue at your door, a regular who stops betting to eat with you |
| 3 Services | gardens replanted, cleaned windows, people out in the evening, deliveries to doors that were dark |
| 4 Automation | robots working again (refurbished, bought for your kitchen), the Dome's ribs lit at night |
| 5 Beyond the kitchen | better seeds from the lab, a Landing Day that feels like a real festival, a town that is proud of itself |

People change too. The NPCs of the Low Ring get small stories that move with the revival, for example a dream-booth
regular who becomes your dishwasher at stage 2 and runs a stall by stage 4. Addiction is shown with respect, as
something people come back from, never as a joke.

## Why the town has borders
The whole town lives under the Dome. Its wall reaches the ground all round, and behind it the Storm Ring stands on
every horizon. Each edge of the map is the same wall told differently: the Wreck sealed in the wall (west), the
Wildbelt sanctuary along the wall (south), the wall running into the lake with a gate for cleaning boats (east), and
the sealed tunnel door to Haven Eight, cargo only (north). Details in `docs/town-plan.md`, section 3.

## Later releases (not v1)
- **Relearning repair:** a mechanic role. The players learn the lost trade, fix their own kitchen robots and then the
  town's, and each repaired robot brings its street a little further back.
- **The seed vault:** the Albatross still holds the ship's original seed library, sealed since the crash. Opening it
  brings back seeds as good as the first harvest.
- **Going outside:** a gate in the Dome, a suit, short trips into the storm-blasted land to find wild seeds and lost
  supplies. The lab crosses and breeds them into better crops. (In game terms only: crop properties computed by the
  sim, never real-world chemistry or biology instructions, per AGENTS.md.)
- **Other Havens:** the tunnel to Haven Eight opens for travel.
- **The rumour:** is the Storm Ring really still deadly, or does the Council keep the doors shut? (Open question 3.)

## What the story gives the game
- **A goal you can see:** progress is the street changing around you, not only a number.
- **A reason for every dark corner:** broken robots, bad food and the Low Ring are the starting state the players
  fix, not decoration.
- **A reason for every landmark:** the Wreck is the landing, the Wildbelt is the animals, Greenhouse Hill is the
  seeds, the Dome is shelter and cage, the Low Ring is the price of the Long Fade.
- **NPC lines:**
  - "My grandmother ate real tomatoes every day. Every day!"
  - "Up there they call it the Sail. Down here it's the Lid."
  - "Free food every day, and I still queue for yours. Funny, isn't it."
  - Broken robot: "Welcome to Haven Nine. Welcome to Haven Nine. Welc—"
  - Later, the same robot, repaired: "Welcome back to Haven Nine."
