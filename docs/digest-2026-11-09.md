# Digest: the 24 h autonomous run (2026-11-08 23:00 to 2026-11-09 23:00)

You said "make it worth it" and "it should feel good". Here is what changed, what to try first, and what needs you.

## Try this first (10 minutes)
1. Pull `main` and build, or run the existing build in `game/Builds/Windows` after rebuilding: `Worktown.exe -host`.
2. The **first-shift tutorial** starts by itself. It walks you through every station, with a highlighted target and
   an arrow. F4 skips it; "Play the tutorial" in the What's-new banner (Shift+F2) or the F1 menu replays it.
3. Press **F2** for the feel panel and switch **Preset A / B** while walking and picking things up. Write "A" or "B"
   into `docs/human-inbox.md`.
