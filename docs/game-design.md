# Worktown game design (DRAFT v0, 2026-11-10, for the human's review)

Status: draft from the brainstorm of 2026-11-10. It becomes binding for every spec once the human approves it.

## The fantasy in one sentence
**Together, turn a small stall into the town's number 1: "Wild Joe's" from the street corner to the main square.**

## Pillars
1. **The work feels good.** Every profession has a hands-on core that is fun on its own and stays fun when you are big.
2. **We build something together.** A crew owns a business with a name, a sign and a story. Progress belongs to the
   crew and to each person.
3. **Status is earned, never bought.** Growth needs money **and** proof (big jobs, critics, reputation).
4. **Few rules, many connections.** Depth comes from professions meeting in the market, not from more menus.
5. **A peaceful, native-vegan town (D27, D28).** Competition is economic and playful. No crime, no weapons, no harm.

## Three layers
| Layer | Time scale | What you do | What it gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hands** (your profession) | seconds to minutes | cook, repair, deliver, sell | stars, tips, streaks, profession XP |
| **Business** (the crew) | days to weeks | grow, hire, invent signature products, choose the location | money, reputation, tiers, upgrades |
| **Town** (everyone) | seasons | supply each other, compete for guests and spots | rankings, trophies, fame |

## The five numbers (everything moves these)
**Quality**, **speed**, **price**, **location** and **reputation**. Every decision moves at least one of them. No
other hidden numbers matter for success.

## The climb (business tiers)
| Tier | Place | To get here | What opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Stall | a street corner | start | 3 dishes, one cook station, self-service |
| 2 Shop | a small shop (today's kitchen) | money + rating 3★ over a week | full kitchen, register, menu board, 1 employee |
| 3 Restaurant | a bigger place in a busy district | money + one big job passed (the stadium rush) | tables, 3 employees, food truck, signature dishes |
| 4 Main square | the best spot in town | money + the critic's review + top 3 in the season ranking | the flagship, a second location, the town paper features you |

- **Upkeep is real.** Rent, wages and stock cost money whether you are open or not.
- **Reputation decays** if you stop, so going down is possible.

## How professions connect (only through the market)
- The grocer sells to the restaurant.
- The mechanic repairs the delivery scooters and food trucks.
- The delivery service carries restaurant food into the districts.
- Later: a farm or tofu maker supplies the grocer.

A crew can specialise (just a restaurant) or integrate (its own grocery: cheaper, better ingredients, a real edge).
Nobody has to master everything; NPC businesses fill every role nobody plays.

## Growing changes what you do
- **Small:** you do everything.
- **Medium:** employees take over the boring parts (washing, the register).
- **Big:** you run the crew, design signature dishes, and do the big jobs yourself.

Menus stay small; choices stay clear.

## Competition and status
- **NPC rival restaurants** in town make the climb a race, even alone or in co-op.
- **Seasonal rankings** per category (restaurant, delivery, workshop, ...). Seasons reset the ranking, never the
  business.
- **Visible status:**
  - the sign grows;
  - the location improves;
  - "restaurant of the week" in the town paper;
  - NPCs talk about you;
  - trophies in the shop;
  - crew titles.

## What we deliberately do not do
- Crime, weapons, violence, sabotage of other crews.
- Spreadsheet management.
- Pay-to-win status.

## How we build it (order)
1. This document, approved by the human.
2. **A climb simulation in /sim:** play "Wild Joe's" for 30 days against NPC rivals in numbers only. Tune until every
   tier takes real effort and there is no shortcut.
3. **Slice 1, playable:** a restaurant crew climbs tiers 1–4 against NPC rivals (in co-op sessions).
4. **The second profession that connects** (grocery or delivery).
5. **The persistent town** with the backend (O4).
